Roblox screening that stays inside Discord.

Collapse checks each member's Roblox account against Rotector. It finds the account through Bloxlink or the username in their nickname, posts anything flagged to your moderation channel, and leaves the decision to a moderator.

Add Collapse to your server → Read the guide

Your moderators don't open another website. Every case and every decision stays in Discord.

What happens when a member joins

  1. Find the member's Roblox account

    If they've verified through Bloxlink, Collapse uses that account. Otherwise it reads the Roblox username at the end of their nickname, written as (@username).

  2. Check the account

    Collapse sends it to Rotector using your server's own Rayward key. It also checks whether Collapse already banned the same Roblox account here under another Discord account.

  3. Show it to a mod

    Anything flagged is posted in your mod channel with Ban and Dismiss buttons. Only your mod role can press them.

Built for Discord moderation

Every server brings its own Rayward key and, if it wants one, a Bloxlink key.

Account lookupBloxlink verification when the server adds a Bloxlink key, otherwise the (@username) tag in the nickname.
ScreeningRotector flag data through the Rayward API, with batched lookups and retries that wait longer each time.
Ban evasionMatches a member's Roblox account against the bans Collapse made in your server.
ReviewCases in a channel you choose, with Ban and Dismiss buttons that only the mod role can press.
Sweeps/sweep start checks every member. It carries on after a restart and stays inside Bloxlink's daily limit.
RecordsA summary channel for sweeps, an optional forum with one thread per detection, and a CSV export from /detections.
StorageSQLite, with API keys encrypted. Rotector's reasons are deleted after 24 hours.

Collapse will

  • Post every flagged account to your mod channel.
  • Check Rotector again when a mod presses Ban.
  • Retry failed lookups, then post the member as Unverified.
  • Delete Rotector's reasons after 24 hours.
  • Record each ban and the mod who approved it.

Collapse won't

  • Ban anyone unless a mod presses Ban.
  • Ban while dry run is on, which it is on every new server.
  • Call an unflagged account safe.
  • Keep Rotector's reasons past 24 hours.

What a case's colour means

The stripe on the left of each case. Sand is the easiest to spot on Discord's dark theme, so it marks the most urgent.
