Documentation and FAQ

Collapse checks the Roblox account behind each member of your server against every flag source on Rayward (Rotector, RCR, TASE, RAB, Okappiki and ServerSweep) and posts the ones any of them has flagged to your mod channel. Your mods decide what happens to each one.

What you need

Before you start, get a Rayward API key by signing in at rayward.app. Collapse uses it to look accounts up on all of Rayward's sources: Rotector, RCR, TASE, RAB, Okappiki and ServerSweep. Each server uses its own key.

A Bloxlink server key is optional. You can create one on the Bloxlink developer dashboard. With it, Collapse can use the Roblox account a member verified through Bloxlink. Without it, Collapse can only find accounts through nickname tags like owl (@example_user).

You'll also want a role for the moderators who decide cases, and a channel where the cases should appear.

Setting up

  1. Add Collapse to your server from the Support page.
  2. Run /setup. This needs Manage Server, or your server's Master or Configurator role if it has set them.
  3. Click Rayward Key and paste your key. If you have a Bloxlink key, add it with Bloxlink Key.
  4. Choose the mod role and the mod channel.
  5. Choose who can run sweeps. Pick a role in the panel, or add specific people under More Settings → Trigger Users, or both.
  6. Click Test & Finish. Collapse tries each key and checks that it can post in your channels, ban members, and sits high enough in the role list.

Collapse starts checking members who join as soon as the key, mod role, mod channel and sweep trigger are set. Test & Finish tells you whether anything will fail when it does.

Discord only lets Collapse ban members whose highest role is below Collapse's own role. Drag its role above your members' roles in Server Settings → Roles.

More Settings also has a summary channel for sweep results, a forum channel to use as a detection log, and an appeal link for banned members. All three are optional. Run /config to open the same panel again later.

Handling a case

Each flagged account is posted in your mod channel as a case, like the example on the home page. It shows the member, their Roblox account, which source flagged it and with what status, any other sources that flag it too, whether the account came from Bloxlink or a nickname tag, and the source's reasons when it gives any.

Only members with your mod role can press the buttons:

  • Ban first looks the account up on every source again. If any source still flags it, Collapse sends the member the ban DM, if it's on, then bans them and records who approved it. If none of them flags the account any more, nobody is banned and the case closes. If a source can't be reached, nothing happens and the case stays open.
  • Dismiss closes the case and does nothing to the member.

Either way the buttons are removed and the case is marked resolved, with the mod who decided it. While dry run is on, Ban records what would have happened. Nobody is banned and no DM is sent.

Rayward's terms don't allow its data to be kept for more than 24 hours, so after 24 hours Collapse removes the Details field (the source's reasons) from the case. The rest of the case stays.

How members are checked

Collapse checks a member when they join, when a sweep reaches them, and when a mod runs /check on them.

First it finds their Roblox account. A Bloxlink verification is used if you've added a Bloxlink key and the member has one. If not, Collapse reads the (@username) tag at the end of their server nickname. A member with neither is skipped.

Next, if Collapse banned that Roblox account in your server in the last 30 days under a different Discord account, the member goes straight to your mods as ban evasion. Bans made outside Collapse don't count here, because Collapse only knows about the bans it made, and it deletes its ban records after 30 days.

Otherwise Collapse looks the account up on all six sources and acts on the most serious answer. A flag from any source goes to your mods, and a source that couldn't answer means the member is tried again, even if the others say unflagged.

Most serious answerCollapse does
UnflaggedNothing
ConfirmedPosts a case
Flagged, Mixed, Queued, Provisional, Redacted, Awaiting Human ReviewPosts a case
Past OffenderRecords it without posting anything, unless report only is on

If a lookup fails or times out, Collapse tries again, leaving longer gaps each time. With the default settings that's five more attempts over about 15 minutes. If they all fail, the member is posted as Unverified.

Sweeps

A sweep checks every member of your server, which is useful after setup or after the sources add new flags. Your sweep triggers start one with /sweep start.

Each server runs one sweep at a time. /sweep status shows how far it has got, /sweep abort stops it, and /sweep resume restarts one that was paused. If the bot restarts during a sweep, the sweep continues on its own afterwards.

Every Bloxlink key can make 2,000 lookups a day. A sweep stops short of that so that members who join later in the day can still be checked, and if it runs out it pauses until midnight UTC. Sweep notices like this one go to your summary channel, or the mod channel if you haven't set one. On big servers a sweep can take more than one day, and Collapse tells you at the start when it will.

When a sweep finishes, the summary goes to your summary channel (or the mod channel if you haven't set one), and by DM to whoever started it:

Sweep #3 complete · 1234 members, 14m07s
Not flagged: 1120
No linked account: 87
Past offender: 4
Sent to review: 21
Unverified: 2
Rotector, RCR, TASE, RAB, Okappiki and ServerSweep via Rayward

Sweeps only open cases. Each ban still needs a mod to press Ban.

Lists and logs

/reviews lists the cases that are still open, ten per page. /detections lists everything Collapse has detected in your server in the last 30 days, resolved or not. You can get it as a CSV file to open in a spreadsheet, or as a list of Roblox usernames.

Collapse deletes its own records 30 days after they were last updated. For a record that stays put, choose a forum channel as the detection log under /config → More Settings. Every detection gets its own thread there, and later decisions don't change it. The threads leave out the sources' reasons, because Collapse can't go back and remove them after 24 hours. If the forum has tags, Detection Log Tags lets you pick one for each kind of detection.

Safety modes and ban DMs

These switches are under /config → Safety Modes. Changes apply straight away.

SwitchStartsWhat it does
Dry RunOnBan presses are recorded, and nobody is banned. Collapse asks you to confirm before turning it off.
Report OnlyOffDetections are posted as notices without buttons, and your mods act on them themselves.
Ban DMOnMembers get a DM just before they're banned.
DM Sweep StarterOnWhoever started a sweep gets its summary by DM.

The ban DM names the server, the member's Roblox account, the source that flags it and its status, and links to where they can appeal with that source. Ban checks again first, so the DM names the source that flags the account at that moment. A ban for ban evasion gets a different DM that doesn't name a source, because that ban comes from Collapse's own records. If a member has DMs closed, they're banned anyway.

To give members somewhere to appeal to you, add an appeal link under /config → More Settings → Appeal Link. It can be an appeals server invite such as discord.gg/yourappeals, or a form. The ban DM then ends with "You can appeal this ban here:" followed by your link. Clear the box to remove it.

Questions

Can Collapse ban someone without a mod?

No. Collapse has no automatic bans and no setting to turn them on.

Nothing happens when people join.

Run /config and check that the Rayward key, mod role, mod channel and a sweep trigger are all set, then click Test & Finish to see what's failing. Members without a Bloxlink verification or a (@username) nickname tag are skipped.

A mod pressed Ban and the member is still here.

Check whether dry run is still on, and read the reply the mod got: no source may flag the account any more, or a source may not have answered. If none of those, Collapse's role is probably below the member's highest role, or Collapse doesn't have Ban Members.

A member says their flag is wrong.

Collapse repeats what the source that flagged them says and can't change it. Your mods can unban them in Discord, and the member can appeal the listing with that source, which their ban DM names and links. If you've set an appeal link, their ban DM already pointed them to you.

What if Rayward, a source or Bloxlink is down?

Collapse keeps trying for about 15 minutes. After that, the members it couldn't check are posted as Unverified so your mods can look at them.

What happens to my data if I remove Collapse?

Your API keys are deleted straight away, and everything else Collapse stored about your server is deleted 30 days later. Even while Collapse is in your server, it deletes cases, ban records and logs 30 days after they were last updated. You can ask in the support server to have it deleted sooner.

Can Collapse see IP addresses?

No. Discord doesn't give bots IP addresses. Collapse spots ban evasion by matching Roblox accounts.
