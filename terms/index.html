Terms of service

These terms apply to anyone who adds Collapse to a Discord server or uses it there. Last updated 9 October 2026.

What Collapse is

Collapse is a Discord bot run by the Collapse team (see the Team page). It finds the Roblox account linked to each member of a server and checks it against the flag data of every source on the Rayward API: Rotector, RCR, TASE, RAB, Okappiki and ServerSweep. It posts flagged accounts to the server's moderators, who decide what to do. Collapse never bans anyone unless a moderator presses Ban.

Using Collapse

If you add Collapse to a server, you're responsible for how it's used there, including every ban your moderators approve.

  • A flag from any source is a safety signal, not proof that someone did something wrong. Your moderators should look at each case and use their own judgement before acting on it.
  • Don't describe an account no source has flagged as safe. It only means nothing has been found about it yet.
  • Don't use Collapse or anything it shows you to harass, threaten or single out anyone.
  • Follow Discord's and Roblox's terms, and the law where you and your members are.

Flag data

Flag data comes from Rotector, RCR, TASE, RAB, Okappiki and ServerSweep, through Rayward. The Collapse team doesn't control any of them and can't change or remove a listing. It can be wrong or out of date. Anyone who thinks a listing about them is wrong can appeal it with the source that listed them: Rotector, RCR, TASE, RAB, Okappiki and ServerSweep. RAB has no appeal form, so the link is its website.

Rayward's terms say its data can't be kept for more than 24 hours. Collapse removes the sources' reasons from cases after 24 hours, and checks every source again before any ban goes through. For the same reason, you may use what Collapse shows you only to moderate your own server. Don't copy, publish, sell or pass on flag data from Collapse, including the files /detections produces.

Your API keys

Each server connects its own Rayward key, and optionally its own Bloxlink key. By adding a key you agree to that service's terms, including Rayward's terms. Collapse stores keys encrypted and uses them only for your server's checks.

Availability and liability

Collapse is provided as it is, without any guarantee that it will be available, accurate or suitable for your server. It depends on Discord, Rayward and its sources, Bloxlink and Roblox, and stops working when they do. As far as the law allows, the Collapse team isn't liable for losses that come from using Collapse, from bans your moderators approve, or from errors in third-party data.

Stopping

You can stop using Collapse at any time by removing it from your server. The Collapse team can stop Collapse working in a server that breaks these terms, or stop running Collapse altogether.

Changes and contact

If these terms change, the new version goes on this page with a new date, and is announced in the support server. Continuing to use Collapse after that means you accept them. Questions go to the support server too.
