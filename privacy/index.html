Privacy policy

What Collapse collects about the members of the servers it's in and the people who run them, why, and who it's shared with. Last updated 9 October 2026.

What Collapse collects

About server members, when they're checked:

  • their Discord user ID and server nickname;
  • the Roblox username and ID linked to them, found through Bloxlink or a (@username) nickname tag;
  • the result of the check, and for flagged accounts, which sources flag it, their statuses and their reasons;
  • if they're banned through Collapse: when, which moderator approved it, and whether the ban DM was delivered.

About the people who set Collapse up and moderate with it: their Discord user ID next to the settings they change, the sweeps they start and the cases they decide.

About each server: its ID, the roles and channels chosen in /config, its Rayward and Bloxlink keys (stored encrypted), and its appeal link.

Collapse doesn't read messages. It only receives server and member information from Discord.

Why

This information is used to run the checks a server's admins have set up, to show cases to its moderators, to stop the same account being checked or banned twice, to spot a banned Roblox account that returns on a new Discord account, and to keep a record of each ban and who approved it. It isn't used for anything else, and it isn't sold.

Who it's shared with

  • Rayward, which provides the flag data of Rotector, RCR, TASE, RAB, Okappiki and ServerSweep: the Roblox IDs being checked, sent with the server's own Rayward key. See Rayward's privacy policy.
  • Bloxlink, if the server added a Bloxlink key: the Discord user IDs of the members being checked, and the server's ID.
  • Roblox: the Roblox usernames and IDs Collapse looks up, to match names to IDs and fetch avatar pictures for cases.
  • Discord: cases, summaries and ban DMs are sent through Discord and appear in the channels the server chose.

How long it's kept

The sources' reasons and their full responses are deleted after 24 hours, as Rayward's terms require. After that, Collapse keeps only the status name as part of its record of what happened, and removes the reasons from the case messages it posted.

Everything else Collapse records about members and moderators is deleted 30 days after it was last updated: cases, ban records, the log of who changed settings and decided cases, failed lookups, and sweep results. Collapse checks for old records every few minutes and deletes them, and nothing is kept longer than 30 days. This means Collapse can only spot ban evasion for 30 days after a ban.

A server's settings and API keys are kept while Collapse is in the server. When Collapse is removed from a server, its API keys are deleted straight away and everything else stored about the server is deleted 30 days later. If Collapse is added back within those 30 days the data is kept, but the keys have to be entered again.

Your choices

If you're a member of a server that uses Collapse, its moderators decide what happens to your account there, so contact them first. If a source has listed your Roblox account, appeal the listing with that source: Rotector, RCR, TASE, RAB, Okappiki and ServerSweep. The Collapse team can't change a listing.

To ask what Collapse holds about you, or to have it corrected, contact the Collapse team in the support server. On request we delete everything we hold about you, in every server, including any ban record, so a server's moderators will no longer have that record. Case messages already posted in a server's channels belong to that server, and its moderators can delete them. Server admins can ask in the same place for all of their server's data to be deleted sooner than 30 days.

Discord doesn't allow accounts for anyone under 13, and Collapse isn't meant for them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date, and is announced in the support server. Questions about privacy go there too.
